AI grid intelligence · Delhi → all-India

Forecasting the power demand of the Indian grid.

Gridalytics turns free, public grid telemetry into live electricity-demand forecasts — at 5-minute, hourly and daily resolution — and scales the same pipeline from the Delhi grid to every Indian state.

Champion models are leak-free and reported against a persistence baseline — honest numbers, no inflated accuracy.
