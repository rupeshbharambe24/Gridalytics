# Prediction-interval calibration

Out-of-sample coverage of each served model's **90% prediction interval** (quantile models at &alpha; 0.05 / 0.95), measured on the `holdout` rows the served artifact never trained on. Generated by `scripts/evaluate_calibration.py` (values aggregated across runs — bulk re-runs hit Open-Meteo throttling, but each coverage figure is a deterministic out-of-sample measurement).

- **Coverage** — % of actual values that fell inside the interval (target ~90%).
- **Width** — mean interval width as % of mean demand (sharpness; lower is tighter).
- **MAPE** — point-forecast error on the same holdout (sanity).

**Summary:** 24 served models, nominal 90%; mean empirical coverage **80.4%**, 10/24 within &plusmn;5 pts. Most models are *under*-covered — the 90% intervals are **over-confident** (too tight), worst at Maharashtra (30%). The quantile bands should be widened / recalibrated (a coverage-tuned &alpha; or a conformal wrap) before any interval is presented as a 90% guarantee. Delhi's three resolutions use the separate Delhi pipeline and are not covered here (follow-up).
